About

A bit about me

I'm finishing my B.Sc. in Computer Engineering (Technische Informatik) at TH Köln, with a focus on networks, distributed systems and software systems.

Java is the language I know best and the foundation for everything I learned after it; Python comes right behind. At TÜV Rheinland I used Python to build a backend that automatically pulls the relevant fields out of PDFs with Azure Document Intelligence and the OpenAI API.

Since February 2026 my focus has been security for industrial networks. In the Cybersecurity / OT Security team of TH Köln's process control lab, I analyse PROFINET traffic with Wireshark, configure industrial network components like Fortigate and Moxa EDR, and check BSI threat scenarios against a real test topology.

I've worked alongside my studies since 2020: at the front desk of a hotel with more than 80 rooms (where I also looked after the WiFi and small tech issues), in hardware quality at Restart and as a developer at TÜV Rheinland. What I enjoy everywhere is the same: understanding how a system really works, then putting that into practice.
